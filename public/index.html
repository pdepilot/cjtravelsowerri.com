How a payment plan works

A simple, four-step process

  1. 1
    Choose your trip Select flights, hotel or package on Travelbookings.
  2. 2
    Submit an application Complete a short form with your details and travel dates.
  3. 3
    Get a decision Our financing partner reviews your application and confirms terms.
  4. 4
    Pay over time Once approved, repay in agreed instalments and travel as planned.
