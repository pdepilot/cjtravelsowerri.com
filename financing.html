What travel financing is

Spread the cost of eligible travel.

Travel financing lets you pay for an eligible trip over an agreed period instead of paying the full amount at once. CJ Travels does not lend money itself — we connect you with independent licensed lending partners who assess your eligibility and set their own terms.

Eligible travel costs

Flights, hotel stays and holiday packages booked through CJ Travels may qualify, subject to the partner's criteria.

Flexible repayment periods

Explore plans over 3, 6, 9 or 12 months. The final term is confirmed by the lending partner.

Independent assessment

Each partner carries out its own affordability and eligibility checks before deciding whether to offer terms.

Your data stays safe

We never ask for bank passwords, card PINs or online banking credentials. Ever.

Eligibility

What partners may look for.

Every lending partner sets its own criteria. The points below are typical considerations, not guarantees of eligibility.

18 years or older

You must be an adult in your country of residence.

Valid government ID

A national ID, passport or driving licence may be required.

Verifiable income

Proof that you can reasonably afford the repayments.

Supported country

Residency in a country where the lending partner operates.

Eligibility criteria, documentation requirements and affordability rules are set solely by each independent licensed lending partner. CJ Travels does not decide who qualifies for financing and does not guarantee that any enquiry will result in an approved loan.
Responsible borrowing

Borrow only what you can comfortably repay.

Financing is a financial commitment. Before you consider any offer from a lending partner, take time to understand the total cost and make sure the repayments fit comfortably within your budget.

  • Borrow only what you need for eligible travel costs.
  • Review the total amount repayable, not just the monthly instalment.
  • Make sure repayments fit your income and other commitments.
  • Missing payments can affect your credit record and may incur fees.
  • Contact the lending partner early if your circumstances change.
  • Consider saving first if you are not certain you can repay.

Who provides the financing?

CJ Travels is a travel agency, not a lender. Financing is provided by independent, licensed lending partners who are solely responsible for assessing applications, setting rates and fees, and issuing loan agreements. Your contract will be with the lending partner, not with CJ Travels.

All financing is subject to the lending partner's eligibility assessment, affordability checks and approval. CJ Travels does not approve, decline or guarantee any application and does not receive or set the interest rate you are offered.
